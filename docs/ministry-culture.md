# The Ministries — FREAK Contributor Culture

*Culture, not contract. Nothing here overrides the bible, the conformance
audit, `AGENTS.md`, or `DOCUMENTATION_AUTHORITY.md`. If this doc and one of
those disagree, this doc loses.*

FREAK organizes stewardship as **ministries**: standing circles of care, not
ranks. You join a ministry by doing its work well, and you keep it by
reviewing others kindly. There are no titles and no elections — the commit
history remembers who showed up.

## The five ministries

- **Ministry of the Compiler** — lexer through codegen, borrow rules, error
  voices. Virtue: precision. A wrong diagnostic here misleads every pilot.
- **Ministry of the Shelves** — stdlib, runtime, Hangar packages. Virtue:
  restraint. A small correct module beats a grand half-tested one.
- **Ministry of the Record** — docs, history, conformance notes. Virtue:
  honesty. Status claims must never exceed executable evidence.
- **Ministry of the Gate** — CI, installers, release plumbing. Virtue:
  vigilance. Green means green on every platform, not just yours.
- **Ministry of Welcome** — issues, labels, first reviews, good-first-issues.
  Virtue: warmth. Every regular was once confused by `pilot` vs `task`.

## How we work

1. **Earnestness over edge.** Criticize code sharply, people never. Cynicism
   about the anime identity is out of scope — the aesthetic is load-bearing.
2. **Small sorties.** One concern per branch, short-lived branches, frequent
   commits. A reverted small PR is a story; a reverted epic is a tragedy.
3. **Leave the map better.** Every PR that changes behavior updates the docs
   that describe it. Lore stays below tech: flavor never buries instructions.
4. **The mission continues after merge.** Watch your PR's follow-ups, answer
   the confused comment a month later, tend your ministry's good-first-issues.

*"The mission doesn't end just because you closed the PR." — CONTRIBUTING.md*
